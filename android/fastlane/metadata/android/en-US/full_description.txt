NyxPlay streams games and the complete desktop from your Windows PC to Android phones, tablets, TVs and Chromebooks with low latency.

Works with NyxPlay Host, Apollo and Sunshine.

Highlights:
- Native resolution streaming with virtual display support (NyxPlay Host / Apollo)
- Native multi-touch, touchpad and mouse modes, custom virtual buttons and controllers
- The on-screen keyboard opens automatically when you tap a text field on the host (NyxPlay Host)
- Correct characters for every keyboard layout, including Turkish ğ ü ş ı ö ç İ
- H.264, HEVC and AV1, HDR, 5.1/7.1 surround sound
- Gamepads with rumble and motion support

NyxPlay is free and open source software (GPL-3.0) based on Artemis and Moonlight.
